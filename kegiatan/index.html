OUR ACTIVITIES

Explore the enriching daily activities and special events at TK Victoria Montessori School that nurture creativity, curiosity, and character in every child.

Learn More 🎓

Daily Activities

🧮

Do the Math

Children learn basic math concepts like counting, addition, and subtraction through fun activities.

📚

Story Time

Teachers read inspiring stories to improve language skills and build character.

🎹

Playing Musical Instruments

Children learn to play the pianica, developing musical skills and creativity.

🌱

Gardening Time

Children learn to plant mung beans, water the plants, and observe the natural growth process.

Special Events & Moments

Kartini Day Celebration

Children wear traditional costumes and learn about national heroes.

Field Trip

Educational visits to explore and understand the real world more closely.

Marine Life Education

Children learn about marine life by directly observing sea creatures at BXSEA.

Imlek Tradition Introduction

Children learn about the culture and traditions of Imlek through fun activities.

Public Transport Education

Children learn about public transportation by taking a train ride from Poris to the airport.

Police Profession Education

Children learn about the role of police officers in maintaining security and order in society.
