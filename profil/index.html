About Our School

Our History

TK Victoria Montessori School didirikan dengan semangat untuk menyediakan pendidikan usia dini yang menyenangkan dan bermakna. Sejak berdiri, kami telah berkomitmen membantu anak-anak berkembang dalam lingkungan yang aman, penuh kasih sayang, dan eksploratif.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a superior educational institution, with global diversity in services and learning processes through the Montessori Method.

Mission: Growing the character of students to be superior in communicating and interacting between cultures, as well as creating an educational environment that respects culture and diversity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the advantages of Montessori education?

Montessori education provides a unique learning environment that fosters creativity, independence, and a love for learning.

How can I enroll my child?

You can enroll your child by contacting our school or visiting the Contact page to fill out the registration form.

Is there a trial class available?

Yes, we offer trial classes so parents and children can experience our learning environment before enrolling.
